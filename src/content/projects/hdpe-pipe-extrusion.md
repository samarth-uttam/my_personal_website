## Overview

Developing the heating module of a compact, low-throughput HDPE pipe extrusion unit that meets ISO standards, for robotic underground construction and trenchless micro-tunnelling systems.

## Approach

Third-party flow-path geometries were integrated into a compact die assembly with custom heating zones, temperature sensors and thermal insulation, designed for integration into a robotic underground construction system.
