## Overview

Implementing SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) on the Android-based Segway Loomo by integrating ROS and adding a LiDAR sensor with temperature and gas leak sensors. Also developped a android based app with MIT app inventor to fetch data from the sensors and update user. 

## Approach

Selecting and integrating external sensors to improve mapping and localisation, and developing an Android application with MIT App Inventor that connects the robot's navigation to property management tasks. …
