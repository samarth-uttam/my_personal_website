## Overview

Designing a feedback-based hydraulic braking system for a pipe inspection robot used to find cracks and structural faults in natural gas pipelines.

## Approach

Detailed 2D manufacturing drawings to GD&T standards, and a component layout optimised for confined pipeline environments.
