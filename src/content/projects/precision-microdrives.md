## Overview

Designing jigs and fixtures for assembly, and developing test systems with Arduino integration and Python interfaces for easy configuration.

## Approach

Material selection and design optimisation using DFMEA and design-for-manufacturing principles, with rapid prototyping and input from production staff. 

## Validation

Assembly and validation of the testers, GR&R studies for accuracy and repeatability, and full documentation including IQ/OQ/PQ documents.
